About Gyde and Kunskapsskolan Gurgaon

Gyandarshan Eduventures (Gyde) has been reimagining K-12 education in India since 2010. In 2012, Gyde brought Sweden's Kunskapsskolan model to India through a joint venture with Kunskapsskolan AB, making this Gurgaon campus the first in India to run the KED Program. The model is built on personalized learning, individual goal-setting, and teachers who coach rather than instruct. Kunskapsskolan Gurgaon is the venue partner for the Suraasa Teacher Development Center hosted by Gyde.
