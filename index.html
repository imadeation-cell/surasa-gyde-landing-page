About This TDC

The Suraasa Teacher Development Centre (TDC) at Kunskapsskolan Gurgaon is a high-impact "skills lab" where international pedagogy meets real-world Indian classroom practice.
Global-Local Synergy: Learn world-class pedagogy from international professors while practicing in a local Gurgaon classroom context.
The "Coach" Model: Experience the famous Swedish KED philosophy firsthand, learning to coach students rather than just instructing them.
Tangible Skill Mastery: Master 50 core pedagogical competencies through structured, in-person practice and immediate educator feedback.
Elite Networking: Join a monthly collaborative cohort of the region's most ambitious educators and build a lifelong professional circle.
Premier Infrastructure: Train within Kunskapsskolan’s world-class campus, utilizing dedicated tech-enabled classroom learning spaces.
Verified Portfolios: Build a digital portfolio that documents your physical teaching practice, providing tangible proof of your expertise.
About

Gyde and Kunskapsskolan Gurgaon

Gyandarshan Eduventures (Gyde) has been reimagining K-12 education in India since 2010.

In 2012, Gyde brought Sweden's Kunskapsskolan model to India through a joint venture with Kunskapsskolan AB, making this Gurgaon campus the first in India to run the KED Program. The model is built on personalized learning, individual goal-setting, and teachers who coach rather than instruct. Kunskapsskolan Gurgaon is the venue partner for the Suraasa Teacher Development Center hosted by Gyde.
